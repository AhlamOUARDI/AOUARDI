This work focuses on the port-Hamiltonian modeling and structure-preserving discretization of viscous sloshing described by the one-dimensional shallow water equations. A port-Hamiltonian formulation is derived, accounting for viscous dissipation and boundary power exchanges. A structure-preserving finite element discretization is then investigated and implemented using SCRIMP. Finally, the model is extended to a moving rigid tank, leading to a coupled finite/infinite-dimensional port-Hamiltonian formulation.\
